The nucleus is the manager — it contains DNA (genetic instructions) and controls all cell activities. The mitochondria are the power plant — they convert food into energy (ATP) through cellular respiration. The ribosomes are the factories — they build proteins using instructions from the nucleus.\n\nThe endoplasmic reticulum (ER) is the transport highway. Rough ER (with ribosomes attached) transports newly made proteins. Smooth ER makes lipids and detoxifies chemicals. The Golgi apparatus is the post office — it packages proteins and sends them where they are needed.\n\nLysosomes are the cleanup crew — they break down waste, old cell parts, and invaders. They contain digestive enzymes. In plant cells, the vacuole serves a similar waste-storage role while also maintaining water pressure.\n\nToday in our lab, we will observe real cells.
