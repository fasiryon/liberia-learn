Before writing was widespread, all knowledge was passed down through stories, songs, proverbs, and ceremonies. Elders are respected as keepers of wisdom because they carry the community's history in their memories.\n\nLiberian proverbs carry generations of wisdom: 'A tree that cannot stand the wind will be broken by the storm' (you must be resilient). 'The frog does not run in the daytime for nothing' (there is always a reason for unexpected actions). (4) Ask follow-up questions when something is interesting. (5) Thank the person for sharing their knowledge.\n\nGood interview questions: What was this area like when you were young? What games did children play? What was school like? How has the community changed? What traditions are important to preserve? What advice do you have for young people?\n\nOral history matters because it preserves stories that might otherwise be lost. It gives us connection to our roots and helps us understand how our community has changed and grown.
