At Waterside Market you can see a football, a tin of tomato paste, a box of soap and an ice cream cone. They are not flat: they are **solid figures**.

What makes a ball different from a box?

- Accessibility: Market objects are solid figures: a football, a tin, a soap box and an ice cream cone. How is a ball different from a box? · keyboard: Read the text, then Continue.

### Faces, edges and vertices (`parts`)

Learner action: Hold a box and touch a face, an edge and a vertex as you read.

A **face** is a flat or curved surface. An **edge** is where two faces meet. A **vertex** is a corner where edges meet.

A sphere (a ball) has one curved face and no edges or vertices. A cube (a die) has 6 square faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices.

- Accessibility: A face is a surface, an edge is where two faces meet, a vertex is a corner. A sphere has no edges or vertices; a cube has 6 faces, 12 edges and 8 vertices. · keyboard: Read the text, then Continue.
- Offline (FULL_OFFLINE): Real objects are the core modality.

### Meet the five solids (`five-solids`)

Learner action: Open each solid and find a real object like it.

Open each solid. Find an object in the classroom or at home with the same shape.

1. **Sphere** — Like a football: one curved face, no edges, no vertices.
2. **Cylinder** — Like a closed tin: 2 flat circle faces, 1 curved face, 2 curved edges, no vertices.
3. **Cone** — Like a paper hat: 1 flat circle face, 1 curved face, 1 edge, 1 vertex at the point.
4. **Cube** — Like a die: 6 equal square faces, 12 edges, 8 vertices.
5. **Rectangular prism** — Like a soap box: 6 rectangle faces in 3 matching pairs, 12 edges, 8 vertices.

- Hints: Turn the real object over to find hidden faces.
- Accessibility: Sphere: no edges or vertices. Cylinder: 2 flat and 1 curved face. Cone: 1 vertex. Cube: 6 equal squares, 12 edges, 8 vertices. Rectangular prism: 6 rectangles, 12 edges, 8 vertices. · keyboard: Each solid is a button; press Enter to open it.
- Offline (FULL_OFFLINE): Text with real objects; no device needed.

## Misconceptions

- `counts-visible-only` (obj5): Counts only the faces they can see in a picture, saying a cube has 3 faces. → Turn a real box or die over: the hidden faces still count. A cube has 6 faces.
- `edge-vertex-mixup` (obj5): Calls corners edges. → An edge is a line where two faces meet; a vertex is a corner where edges meet.
