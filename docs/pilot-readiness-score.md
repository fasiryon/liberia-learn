# Pilot Readiness Score — How It Works

The Pilot Readiness Score measures how prepared each school is for the LiberiaLearn national pilot. It is a number from 0 to 100, broken into 5 equal parts worth 20 points each.

## Score Components

### 1. Attendance Coverage (20 points)
**Question:** Is the school tracking student attendance regularly?

| Points | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| 20 | Attendance recorded for 80% or more of school days this week |
| 10 | Attendance recorded for 50-79% of days |
| 0 | Less than 50% of days, or no attendance taken |

### 2. Curriculum Adoption (20 points)
**Question:** Are teachers using the curriculum tools to prepare lessons?

| Points | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| 20 | Every active class has at least 1 published lesson in the last 14 days |
| 10 | Some classes have published lessons |
| 0 | No lessons published in the last 14 days |

### 3. Student Engagement (20 points)
**Question:** Are students completing their assigned work?

| Points | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| 20 | 60% or more of assigned work completed this week |
| 10 | 30-59% completion rate |
| 0 | Less than 30% completion |

### 4. Guardian Connection (20 points)
**Question:** Are families connected to the platform?

| Points | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| 20 | 50% or more of students have a guardian linked |
| 10 | 20-49% of students have guardian links |
| 0 | Less than 20% guardian coverage |

### 5. Platform Setup (20 points)
**Question:** Is the school properly configured?

| Points | Criteria |
|--------|----------|
| 5 | School branding set (logo or colors) |
| 5 | Onboarding wizard completed |
| 5 | At least 1 teacher account active |
| 5 | At least 1 class with enrolled students |

## Grade Scale

| Grade | Score Range | Meaning |
|-------|------------|---------|
| A | 90-100 | Fully ready for pilot |
| B | 75-89 | Nearly ready, minor improvements needed |
| C | 60-74 | Making progress, some areas need attention |
| D | 40-59 | Significant setup still needed |
| F | 0-39 | Not yet ready, needs immediate action |

## How to Achieve 100/100

1. Take attendance every school day
2. Generate and publish at least 1 lesson per class every 2 weeks
3. Encourage students to complete at least 60% of assigned work
4. Link guardians for at least half of all students
5. Complete the school onboarding wizard, set branding, add teachers, and enroll students

## Notes for MOE

- Scores update in real time based on actual school activity
- The platform admin dashboard shows all schools ranked by score
- Schools scoring below 60 may need additional support or training
- The score formula can be adjusted by the platform team if MOE priorities change
