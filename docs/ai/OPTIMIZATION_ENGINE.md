# Optimization Engine

## Forecasting Systems

Forecasting may predict learning risk, attendance risk, curriculum coverage risk, intervention demand, queue load, AI cost, or national readiness. Forecasts must include confidence, source data, model version, and limitations.

## Trend Analysis

Trend analysis should use existing analytics, `LearningEvent`, mastery snapshots, assessments, interventions, curriculum usage, and AI cost telemetry. National trend analysis must be aggregate-only.

## Curriculum Optimization

Curriculum optimization may propose:

- objective refinement
- local-context examples
- assessment strengthening
- misconception coverage
- teacher notes
- lesson sequencing changes

Publication remains approval-gated through existing curriculum governance.

## National Intelligence Aggregation

National intelligence must aggregate by county, district, school type, grade, subject, curriculum standard, and time period while suppressing small cohorts and removing raw PII.

## Recommendation Generation

Recommendations must include:

- action proposal
- target scope
- evidence
- risk level
- confidence
- expected outcome
- evaluation plan
- approval requirement

## Explainability Requirements

Every optimization output must explain why it was generated, which signals mattered, what uncertainty exists, and what human review is required.
