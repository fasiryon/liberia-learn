the same player, capability profiles, LabAction kernel and Labs catalogue as
existing labs. All definitions are DRAFT/PENDING and all generated links remain

The source packages and assessment keys remain unchanged. Runtime adaptation
accepts only exact canonical package hashes and binds the authored scene/media
pins to registered DRAFT definitions. It changes interaction routing to
LAB_LAUNCH; it retains scene content, media, fallback prose and raw original
authoring bytes in review evidence. Changed inputs need a fresh binding review.
Runtime source hashes also bind immutable package identity, so a code change
cannot silently reuse an existing review package.

Water is an ideal equilibrium model of pure water near one atmosphere, using
rounded heat capacities and latent heats. The energy coordinate is J/g relative
to ice at -20 C. Melting at 0 C and boiling at 100 C have energy plateaux;
evaporation below boiling is a separate schematic exchange view. Twenty-four
fixed-size samples represent the water throughout; rounded sample counts do not
claim exact mass fractions or measured trajectories. No sealed rigid vessel or
practical measurement certification is claimed.

Fraction operations use integer masks. Unsupported common subdivisions above
24 intervals per whole, arithmetic beyond two wholes or a negative result are
refused. A denominator change explicitly starts a cleared strip; subdivision
preserves all shaded pieces. Independent work suppresses worked paper examples,
explanations and hints and prevents escaping to practice/reset mid-assessment.

County display geometry derives from the delivered geoBoundaries geographic
source. Two original SVG shorelines self-intersected; the numeric adapter
rebuilds all display contours with a maximum simplification tolerance of 0.015
world units and rejects crossings. It preserves the original SVG hash, source
GeoJSON hash, county identities and shared positive-length source-edge graph.
Interior rings remain omitted as in the supplied teaching SVG. This is a
teaching map, not a legal boundary depiction. Attribution and CC-BY-3.0-IGO remain
explicit. That license differs from the asset role's narrow allowlist: an owner
must resolve the policy exception before release. No allowlist was changed.

Paper activities are embedded in the shared control panel, including a printable
numbered blank map and separately listed cards. Independent mode uses neutral
recording instructions and keeps teacher checking material separate. No remote
asset is needed by the models or paper activity after the app is available.

Read-only design reviews identified and corrected liquid motion, surface
crossing, interior vapour-region, quarter-specific check, answer-order and mobile
coaching issues. Browser captures use the dev-only review harness with no
learner evidence recording. Software Chromium captures prove layout and scripted
interaction only; real-device performance, school pilots and human academic
approval remain outstanding. The records intentionally remain DRAFT.
