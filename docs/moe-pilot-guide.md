# LiberiaLearn — MOE Pilot Guide

## What is LiberiaLearn?

LiberiaLearn is a digital education platform built specifically for Liberian schools. It helps school administrators manage their schools, teachers create and deliver curriculum-aligned lessons, students complete their daily work, and guardians stay informed about their children's progress. The platform works on any device with a web browser and is designed for Liberia's internet conditions — students can load lessons at home over WiFi and complete them offline at school.

LiberiaLearn is not a replacement for teachers. It is a tool that makes teachers more effective by handling curriculum organization, attendance tracking, progress monitoring, and parent communication — so teachers can focus on teaching.

## What Can Schools Do on the Platform?

### School Administrator
- Set up school identity, branding, and settings
- Manage teacher accounts and class assignments
- Review and approve curriculum before it reaches students
- Monitor school performance through the Pilot Readiness Score
- Link guardians to students for SMS notifications
- View notification history and audit logs

### Teacher
- Generate curriculum-aligned lessons using the AI-powered curriculum factory
- Schedule lessons to specific classes on specific days ("Today's Work")
- Monitor student progress — see who is active, at risk, or inactive
- Flag students for guardian follow-up
- View weekly schedule with completion rates

### Student
- See today's assigned lessons on a simple, clear dashboard
- Open lessons and read content, objectives, activities, and lab work
- Mark lessons as complete when finished
- Track personal progress, including streak count and subject breakdown
- Work offline — completions sync automatically when internet returns

### Guardian
- Receive SMS notifications when their child is marked absent
- Receive SMS when a teacher flags their child for attention
- Receive weekly progress reports via SMS

## How Does a School Get Started?

1. **School Identity** — Enter school name, county, and principal name
2. **Branding** — Choose school colors and upload a logo
3. **Add Teachers** — Invite teachers with their email addresses
4. **Create Classes** — Set up classes and assign teachers
5. **Ready!** — The school is set up and ready for the pilot

This takes under 10 minutes and can be done by the school administrator without any technical help.

## How Does MOE Monitor School Performance?

The **Pilot Readiness Score** is a 0-100 score that measures how actively each school is using the platform. It has five components, each worth 20 points:

1. **Attendance Coverage** — Is the school taking attendance regularly?
2. **Curriculum Adoption** — Are teachers generating and publishing lessons?
3. **Student Engagement** — Are students completing their assigned work?
4. **Guardian Connection** — Are families linked to the platform?
5. **Platform Setup** — Is the school properly configured?

Schools are graded A through F. The platform admin dashboard shows all schools ranked by score, with color-coded indicators. Schools scoring below 60 may need additional support.

## What Does a Student's Day Look Like?

1. Student opens LiberiaLearn on their phone or school computer
2. They see "Today's Work" — the lessons their teacher has scheduled
3. They tap a lesson to read the content, objectives, and activities
4. When finished, they tap "Mark Complete"
5. If they have no internet, the completion is saved and syncs later

## How Are Parents Kept Informed?

Guardians receive SMS messages (via Africa's Talking) when:
- Their child is marked absent from school
- A teacher flags their child as needing attention
- Weekly progress reports are generated

SMS is only sent to guardians who have opted in. All messages are in English and kept under 160 characters for reliable delivery on basic phones.

## Technical Requirements

- **Device**: Any smartphone, tablet, or computer with a web browser
- **Browser**: Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge (latest version recommended)
- **Internet**: Required for initial login and lesson loading; offline mode available for lesson completion
- **Phone (Guardian SMS)**: Any phone that receives SMS — no smartphone required
