# NR-13 Grade 5-8 Coverage Matrix

Status: authored and validator-complete
Version: `nr13-2026.1`

NR-13 uses Liberia Ministry of Education curriculum authority as the scope
and objective boundary. The instructional materials are an authored upgrade:
each lesson includes explicit instruction, two worked examples, guided release,
independent transfer, misconception repair, remediation, extension, teacher
guidance, home support, evidence recording, and mapped assessment. A lesson
does not pass on word count alone. The shared approval floor is 3,500 combined
standard/block body words plus the structural and semantic checks in
`validateNr13Lesson`.

## Scope decision

NR-13 has 4 grades x 5 authorized delivery subjects x 15 lessons = 300
lessons. The delivery subjects are Mathematics, English, Science, Social
Studies, and Civics.

English is a separate delivery subject from Social Studies. It is stored as
`ENGLISH` for the standalone English coverage audit, while its authority is
the repository's Liberia MOE Language Arts records (`LR-LIT-*`). Its lessons
progress through reading comprehension, vocabulary/context, grammar,
composition, oral language, literature, mechanics, language usage, and
research/information literacy as appropriate to the grade.

Social Studies has its own content, strands, units, objectives, and authority
locators. It is not an English comprehension wrapper. The Grade 5-6 objectives
are traced to the Liberia MOE Social Studies Grade 1-6 curriculum, and Grade
7-8 objectives to the Liberia MOE Social Studies Grade 7-9 curriculum:

- [MOE Social Studies Grade 1-6 archive](https://edumedia-depot.gei.de/server/api/core/bitstreams/3a688268-1392-4fe5-a904-dd4b50df5aeb/content), SHA-256 `5e6330de23f882058415b713cdf9bf28053214cd3e8ea54edb8191e13617a53d`
- [MOE Social Studies Grade 7-9 archive](https://edumedia-depot.gei.de/bitstreams/d65495ce-a08a-416f-96bf-3db00a79b37d/download), SHA-256 `89b1f263f6cd238d4e1c9b31897f85a2ca7cdcece5c78f21ed72f1b8262849bb`

The repository MOE authority codes are defined in
`prisma/seeds/moe-standards.ts`. Social Studies codes use the verified source
locator form `MOE.2011.G{grade}.SOCIAL_STUDIES.U{unit}` because Social Studies
was not present in that seed file. No WAEC code is invented for Grades 5-8.

## Progression and assessment proof

Each lesson carries a prerequisite and next-concept link. Grade 5 begins from
the Grade 4 foundation; Grade 6 links to Grade 5; Grade 7 links to Grade 6;
Grade 8 links to Grade 7 and explicitly bridges into the Grade 9 pathway. The
three lesson stages per unit are concept build, guided application, and
independent transfer. This prevents the same lesson from being copied under
four grade labels.

Each lesson has five deterministic mapped MCQs with four distinct options,
exact answer keys, explanations, and varied answer positions. Unit and term
blueprints contain 10 and 30 questions respectively. Assessment alignment is
validated against the lesson's authority codes and documented misconception.

The read-only audit command is `npm run audit:nr13`. The governed runner is
`npm run run:nr13 -- dry_run`; persistence requires the explicit
`NR13_ALLOW_WRITE=true` gate and is never used against production or staging by
the NR-13 repository gate.
