# Pilot School Selection Criteria

## Recommended School Mix

For a representative pilot, we recommend selecting schools from three settings:

1. **Urban School** (Montserrado County)
   - Strong internet connectivity
   - Computer lab or 1:1 devices available
   - Teachers with smartphone access
   - Expected to achieve highest Pilot Readiness Score

2. **Peri-urban School** (Margibi or Bong County)
   - Intermittent internet connectivity
   - Shared devices or small computer lab
   - At least 1 teacher with smartphone
   - Tests the offline sync capability

3. **Rural School** (Lofa, Nimba, or Grand Bassa County)
   - Limited internet (possibly only mobile data)
   - Minimal devices — may share 3-5 tablets
   - Tests offline-first workflow and SMS guardian notifications
   - May use national blueprint curriculum (allowBlueprintAdoption)

## Minimum Requirements Per School

- At least 1 functioning device with a web browser (smartphone, tablet, or computer)
- At least 1 teacher with a smartphone (for curriculum generation and scheduling)
- Internet access at least once per week (for syncing data)
- School administrator willing to complete the onboarding wizard
- Consent from guardians for SMS notifications (where applicable)

## Rollout Timeline

| Phase | Duration | Activity |
|-------|----------|----------|
| Week 1-2 | Setup | School onboarding, teacher training, guardian linking |
| Week 3-6 | Active Pilot | Daily lesson delivery, attendance tracking, SMS notifications |
| Week 7-8 | Review | Pilot Readiness Score analysis, teacher and student feedback |
| Week 9 | Decision | MOE review meeting — expand, adjust, or halt |

## Success Metrics

- **Pilot Readiness Score**: All pilot schools maintain Grade C or above (60+)
- **Student Engagement**: At least 50% of assigned lessons completed weekly
- **Attendance Tracking**: At least 80% of school days have attendance recorded
- **Guardian Coverage**: At least 30% of students have a linked guardian
- **Teacher Adoption**: At least 1 lesson published per class per week
